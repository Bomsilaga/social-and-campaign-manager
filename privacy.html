Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Applies to https://social-and-campaign-manager.vercel.app

Who operates this service? Boma Social Campaign Manager (the “Service”) is a social-content creation and campaign-management application operated by the Boma Social Campaign Manager team in Australia. For privacy enquiries, deletion requests or account-connection assistance, contact bomsilaga@gmail.com.

This policy explains the application’s current prototype behaviour and how future optional TikTok and Facebook connections would work. A feature described as planned is not active merely because this policy mentions it.

1. Information we handle

Information you provide: Creative prompts, scripts, captions, brand preferences, project titles, project manifests and uploaded images, videos or audio that you select for editing. In the current browser-based editor, uploaded media is processed locally in your browser and is not automatically uploaded to our database. Locally stored preferences remain in your browser until cleared or exported.

Optional AI requests: When you deliberately activate a configured AI service, the text or media necessary to fulfil your request may be transmitted to the selected provider (for example, OpenAI, Anthropic or ElevenLabs). AI features requiring payment are disabled by default and require configuration and confirmation. Applicable provider terms and privacy notices govern their processing.

Optional cloud storage: If the project-storage integration is configured and you choose to save a project, the Service stores a project manifest containing titles, creative text, scene timing, rights references and settings in Supabase. The current integration does not upload full video, photo or audio files as part of a saved manifest.

Optional connected social accounts: If you choose to connect a supported social account after its integration is enabled, the Service may receive an account identifier, display name, authorised profile details, selected Page information, scopes granted and access or refresh tokens as provided by the platform. The Facebook connection implementation protects its stored token using server-side encryption. TikTok login and posting are not yet activated.

Technical data: Hosting and API providers may process essential technical information, such as IP addresses, request timestamps, browser details and security logs, to deliver, maintain and protect the Service.

2. TikTok data and authorisation

When TikTok Login Kit or Content Posting API is later activated and you explicitly authorise access, the Service will request only the permissions necessary for the feature you choose. Intended permissions include basic account information, uploading videos as drafts and, only if authorised and approved, directly publishing user-approved content. Availability is subject to TikTok approval and permissions. We do not use your TikTok account or publish content without your authorisation and a separate approval step.

TikTok access can be withdrawn through your TikTok account's authorised-app settings. Revoking access prevents future authorised requests but may not erase data previously saved to the Service; contact us to request deletion of that data. Our TikTok developer integration is currently in setup and review, not general availability.

3. Purposes and lawful handling

  • Provide video editing, creative drafting and campaign-planning functionality requested by you.
  • Authenticate eligible accounts and, where implemented, retrieve permitted account details to enable account selection.
  • Maintain project manifests and your chosen settings where storage is enabled.
  • Protect account connections, prevent unauthorised access, troubleshoot the Service and address support enquiries.
  • Prepare social media uploads only when separately enabled, authorised and approved.

We do not sell personal information or social-platform account information to advertisers. We do not use third-party footage or another creator’s account information as evidence of permission to republish it.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information is shared only as needed to operate features you activate, including hosting and development infrastructure (Vercel), configured project storage (Supabase), AI providers you choose, and platform APIs such as TikTok or Meta when you authorise their use. These providers may process information in different countries subject to their own terms and the contractual arrangements applicable to the service.

5. Security

We use HTTPS, server-side provider keys and, for the implemented Meta account flow, encrypted token storage. However, this is a developing prototype: the current studio access system uses a shared session token rather than mature multi-user authentication. Do not upload confidential or sensitive content until production-grade authentication, access controls and a full security review are completed. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and deletion

Local editor assets and session data are generally discarded when the browser session or page is closed or reloaded; browser preferences persist until cleared. Where optional project saving is enabled, manifests remain in storage until deleted, the service is reset, or retention practices change. Connected-account tokens, if any, remain until connection removal, invalidation or a deletion request. Technical logs may be retained according to infrastructure-provider policies.

To request access, correction, disconnection or deletion of account-related information and stored project data, email bomsilaga@gmail.com with sufficient details to locate your account. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account owner. We will respond within a reasonable period and explain any data that cannot be immediately deleted because of legal or security requirements.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 18, and we do not intentionally seek their personal information. Contact us if you believe personal information about a child has been provided.

8. Changes

We may revise this policy when new features, providers or legal requirements are introduced. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published. Please review this page before activating a new integration.

9. Contact

Privacy and data requests: bomsilaga@gmail.com.
